Robotics notes

I run the robotics work at STEM Innovation Academy. In 2026 I led the purchase of two Unitree robots for our mechatronics program: a Go2 quadruped named Kepler and an R1 humanoid named Vera. We chose them for full SDK and ROS access, not for demo value. These are the notes I wrote while commissioning them.
